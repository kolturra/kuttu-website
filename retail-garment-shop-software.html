Who it suits, and who it does not

Kuttu is built for one shop on one Windows PC: a saree shop, a readymade store, a showroom, a tailor or a boutique that sells to walk-in customers. It is not an online store and it does not link several branches. If you need that, we will tell you honestly.

Questions

Is Kuttu right for a retail garment shop?

Yes, if you sell over the counter from one shop. It handles stock with barcodes, GST bills, part-payments and balance due, tailoring and alteration orders, and staff pay. It does not run an online store or sync several branches.

Can it handle sarees, readymade garments and fabric in one shop?

Yes. Saree, Readymade and Astar (fabric) each have their own ledger and sale screens, and Consolidated Analytics shows the whole shop in one report.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Your records stay on your own Windows PC and Kuttu works offline. The Online Backup edition adds an hourly copy of your data in the Kuttu cloud.

Can it give my accountant the GST figures?

GST Summary shows the GST on your bills for any period, by application, HSN code and bill, and exports to Excel. It is a summary of your own bills, not a GST return filing.

Which languages does it support?

Every screen can be switched between English, Hindi and Telugu.
