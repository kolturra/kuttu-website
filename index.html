Stitching Measurement

Every customer's measurements, always to hand.

Pick the gender, tap the garment, enter the measurements. Add a blouse, churidar and salwar to the same visit — they all file under one order number. Ordering two churidars? Both fit on the same order, each with its own measurements.

  • Print Bill for the whole order in the same layout as the sale bills, and a Print Preview that stays clean — the measurements are on the cutting slip, not on the customer's copy
  • The orders list shows each garment's amount, discount, advance paid and what is still due, with a running total of money owed. While you edit an order, a live line shows its total items, bill amount and due
  • Printable cutting and tailoring slips for the workshop
  • Delivery status, customer rating and feedback recorded after handover
  • Lining metres used can reduce real Astar Ledger stock through the item's barcode
  • Saree fall, pico, blouse cutting and border work as a service order — choose any combination, and the border fabric type when there is one
  • Blouse workings are ordered on their own: aari, maggam, embroidery, zari, stone, beads, cut work or tassels, in any combination, alongside the blouse itself
  • Alterations are a garment of their own: pick what is being altered (blouse, salwar, kurta, saree and more) from a list, with no body measurements needed, and note the order it was first made on
  • The Item Order list has a Gender column, so a mixed men's and women's order is clear at a glance
  • Give each garment its own name, such as "Red wedding blouse", so a repeat order is easy to tell apart
  • Add a Trial Date between the order date and delivery date for fittings
  • One order can hold both men's and women's garments for the same family — each garment keeps its own gender, and the bill and chit list them together
  • Customer gave a sample garment? Mark it and skip the measurements
  • A Due Soon list sits right on your Home screen: orders that are late show in red, due today in amber, and due within 7 days below — double-click one to jump straight to that order
  • VIP and Premium customers get an extra care note on the cutting slip
  • Merge orders that belong to the same customer into one, with one combined bill

Billing

Proper GST bills, in seconds.

Bill from any sale module or use Print Bill for anything else. Bills carry your shop name, taxable value and CGST/SGST, and can be printed or sent on WhatsApp.

  • GST set per item — only taxable items are taxed
  • One overall discount, as a percentage or in rupees
  • An Advance Payments table on every billing form — Add Payment, Edit Selected, Delete Payment — with any number of part-payments tracked against the bill, oldest first; a payment can never be more than what is due
  • Tick Paid to record the whole due amount as paid, with its own paid date — the advance you took earlier stays separate
  • Every bill ends with a payment history: each payment's date, time, mode and amount, and what was still due after it — on the printed bill, the preview and the WhatsApp message, for sales and stitching orders alike
  • Pick a date in the calendar and the time is kept with it, shown on the bill; every later change of a date is recorded in a date edit log
  • Customer contact, WhatsApp number, order and delivery dates on the bill
  • Barcode-matched items reduce ledger stock exactly like a sale
  • Every bill reads the same way in every billing module: items, taxable value, CGST and SGST, invoice value, advance and balance due
  • A Balance Due list shows every bill with money still to collect
  • Send via WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with the bill ready — you press Send
  • Set your own shop name, GSTIN, address and phone once in Shop Settings — they print on every bill. No GSTIN entered means no GST is charged
  • Bill under more than one GST number: add up to five companies in Shop Settings, each with its own GSTIN, address and bill numbers (such as X-0041 and Y-0017). Each stock item belongs to a company, so scanning its barcode bills it under the right GST number — you never switch anything at the counter. Choose a default company for items without a barcode, and switch any company on or off. In GST Summary, switch between one company and all of them
  • Set the CGST and SGST percentages in Shop Settings. If the rate changes, old bills keep the rate they were charged at
  • Mark each fabric, saree or readymade sale as GST or Non-GST — the whole bill follows your choice, and Non-GST bills show no CGST or SGST
  • Switch GST off for any billing module that doesn't need it — those bills say "GST: Not applicable"
  • QR Code and Online payments record a Transaction ID, printed on the bill
  • Bill-number prefixes for each module are yours to set
  • Set one HSN code for each billing module in Shop Settings, printed on every bill line
  • A GST Summary on Home totals your CGST and SGST by month — per application, per HSN code and bill by bill — ready to export to Excel for your accountant. With several companies, choose one company or all of them; the Excel export writes one file for each company, headed with its name and GSTIN

FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

Is Kuttu only for boutiques?

No. Kuttu is for the whole garment industry: factories, wholesalers, garment showrooms, saree shops, readymade stores, tailoring units and boutiques. It covers stock, GST bills, stitching orders, who cut and stitched what, attendance, salary and reports. It does not yet do challans, e-way bills or production planning, so if you need those, ask us before you buy.

Where is my data stored? Is it safe?

On your own PC, in local files — in a folder you choose, on any drive. The first time you open Kuttu it asks where to keep your data, and you can change it later in System Settings, which also shows where your data, backups and logs are. Nothing is uploaded, saves are atomic, and backups are kept automatically.

Do I need internet?

No. Kuttu runs fully offline. Internet is only used if you choose to share a bill through WhatsApp.

Can I use it for only sarees, or only stitching?

Yes. Each module opens on its own from Home, so use only the ones your shop needs.

Can I bill under more than one GST number?

Yes. In Shop Settings you can add up to five companies, each with its own GSTIN, address, phone and bill numbers. Each stock item belongs to one company, so scanning its barcode bills it under the right GST number. If one basket holds items from two companies, Kuttu prints one invoice for each. A company with no GSTIN charges no GST. Stitching orders go under the company you choose as the default. The GST Summary can show one company or all of them. A shop with one company sees no change.

How many PCs does one license cover?

One license covers one PC. If you want to run Kuttu on another computer, that needs its own license.

How do I buy, and what happens after I pay?

Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 72076 29246. We take your shop details, confirm the payment and send you a private download link for the installer. If you lose the link, contact us and we will send it again. After installing, Kuttu asks you to activate it — see the next question.

How does activation work?

When you first open Kuttu it shows your PC's Machine ID with Email and WhatsApp buttons. Send it to us, and we reply with a license key made for that PC. Paste the key in and Kuttu is activated. A key only works on the PC it was made for, and your license runs for one year from the day we issue it.

What happens after one year? How do I renew?

About 60 days before your license ends, Kuttu shows a red line that scrolls across the Home screen with the date and the days left. To renew, send us your Machine ID again (it's in Home → System Settings), and we reply with a new key. In System Settings, click Renew License, paste the key and Activate — your data and settings stay exactly as they are. If a license ends before you renew, Kuttu asks for a new key when it opens; your records are never deleted or locked away. For the renewal price, contact us on WhatsApp or use the contact section.

Can my staff use it in Hindi or Telugu?

Yes. Every screen can be switched between English, हिन्दी and తెలుగు from the Home screen. Bills and messages sent to your customers stay in English.

What do I need to run Kuttu?

A Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) PC with about 1 GB of free space. You don't need administrator rights or internet. A printer, WhatsApp on the PC and a USB barcode scanner are optional.

What if I change my PC or reinstall Windows?

The license is tied to the PC. If the computer changes, Kuttu shows a new Machine ID — send it to us and we reply with a new key. Copy your data folder across to keep your records.

Do you train our staff?

Yes — we can help in Telugu or Hindi as well as English. Message us on WhatsApp.

Will I get updates? Are they free?

Bug fixes are free for customers who have bought a license. When there's a fix, we send you the new installer. Run it over your existing Kuttu — your data and your license are kept, so you don't enter anything again. Updates never install by themselves, so nothing changes until you choose to. New modules are paid add-ons, and we'll tell you the price before you decide. Each program's version is in its file properties (right-click the file, then Properties, then Details), so you always know what you have. Before updating, it's good practice to copy your data folder as a backup.

What if installation goes wrong?

Message us on WhatsApp or email and we'll help you get running.

What is the refund policy?

There are no refunds. Your purchase covers a one-year license for one PC, so please ask us any questions before you buy. See the Terms and Conditions.

How we handle your details

For how buying and using Kuttu works, see the Terms and Conditions.

  • What we collect when you sign up: your name and email address or mobile number, from the sign-in method you choose (Google or email). If you sign in with email and password, we never see your password.
  • Your profile: after you sign in we also ask for your mobile number, shop or business name, type of shop, city, state, pincode and country, and (if you choose) your GST number. You can view and change them any time from My profile, which also lists your purchases (reference, date, amount, status) and billing details, matched by your verified email address.
  • Deleting your account: sign in, open My profile and choose Delete my account at the bottom. This removes your sign-in and the profile you saved. Records of purchases (payment reference, date and amount) are kept for our accounts and support; write to enquiry@kuttu.org to have those removed as well. A Kuttu license already installed on your PC keeps working.

It does not collect your name, phone number or email for this, and no advertising is built on it. If you tap No, nothing is loaded. To change your answer, clear this site's data in your browser.

  • Who else sees it: the sign-in service that stores accounts (Google Firebase), the payment provider that takes your payment, and the email service that delivers your download link. We do not see your card or UPI details, and we do not sell or rent your details to anyone.
  • If you apply for a job: we collect your name, email, mobile number, the role, any message you write and the resume you upload. Only our hiring team can open them. We use them only to consider you for a role, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.
