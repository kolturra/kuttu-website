What it does for you

Who cut and stitched what

Enter the garments each cutting master or tailor completed each day, and see each person's totals and daily average for any period.

Attendance register

Mark Present, Half day or Absent with In and Out times and a note, with a summary of days worked and absent for any period.

Salary for the whole team

Pay monthly, weekly or daily with leave deducted per day, a bonus where you give one, shift hours for daily-pay staff and a printable salary slip.

Orders and cutting slips

Take orders with measurements, merge orders and print cutting slips for the workshop.

Stock and GST bills

Ledgers for fabric and readymade stock with barcode labels, and GST bills with the balance each customer owes.

One report for everything

Consolidated analytics bring sales, stock value, stitching orders, staff output, expenses, and salary and rent into one page.
