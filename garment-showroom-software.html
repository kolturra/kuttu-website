What it does for you

Stock by type and size

Fixed dropdowns for size, fabric and garment type stop typing mistakes, so your stock and reports always agree.

Barcode labels and scanning

Print barcode labels from the stock ledgers, then scan or type a barcode when billing to fill in the item and price. It works with any USB barcode scanner.

GST bills in seconds

GST is set per item, with one overall discount as a percentage or in rupees. Bills print with taxable value and CGST/SGST and can open in WhatsApp.

Stock that stays in sync

Sell it once and stock updates, with no double entry. Every ledger shows what came in, what went out and what is left.

Restock alerts

Set a minimum stock and reorder level. A Needs Restock list shows what is low or out, with the vendor's name and number.

Balance due and reports

A Balance Due list shows every bill with money still to collect, and consolidated analytics give reports and charts for the whole shop.

Questions

Does it work with a barcode scanner?

Yes. Kuttu prints barcode labels for stock items and works with any USB barcode scanner when billing.

Can I bill under more than one GST number?

Yes. You can add up to five companies in Shop Settings, each with its own GSTIN and address.

Does it need internet at the counter?

No. Kuttu runs on your own Windows PC and works without internet.
