What it does for you

Stock by item

Ledgers for fabric, sarees and readymade garments show what came in, what went out and what is left, with barcode labels.

GST bills

Bills carry your shop name, GSTIN, taxable value and CGST/SGST, with GST set per item. You can bill under up to five GST numbers.

The balance each buyer owes

Track part-payments with their dates and modes, and see a Balance Due list of every bill with money still to collect.

Any-item invoices

Print Bill makes an invoice for any item or service, with per-item GST, discount, advances and WhatsApp sharing.

GST Summary for your accountant

See the GST on all your bills for any period, by application, HSN code and bill, and export it to Excel.

Best customers and vendors

Consolidated analytics show top customers, top vendors, best sellers by barcode and who still owes you money.

Questions

Can I bill under more than one GST number?

Yes. You can add up to five companies in Shop Settings, each with its own GSTIN, address and bill numbering.

Yes. Kuttu runs on your own Windows PC and works without internet.
