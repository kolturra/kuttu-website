What it does for you

Stitching orders with measurements

Several garments for one customer sit under one order number, each with its own measurements, amount, discount, advance paid and balance due.

Cutting and tailoring slips

Print slips for the workshop with the measurements, so the tailor has everything and the customer's bill stays clean.

Who cut and stitched what

At day end enter how many garments each cutting master or tailor completed, and see each person's days worked, total garments and daily average for any period.

Attendance register

Mark Present, Half day or Absent with In and Out times and a note, with a summary of days worked and days absent for any period.

Salary and piecework

Salary & Rent picks up each person's days worked or absent from the register, so payday becomes arithmetic. Staff names are typed once and shared across the modules.

Delivery and money due

Track delivery status, the advance paid and what is still due, with a running total of money owed on the orders list.

Questions

Can I track which tailor stitched which garment?

Yes. The Employee Tracker records the garments each cutting master or tailor completed each day, and totals them for any period.

Does it calculate salary from attendance?

Salary & Rent picks up each person's days worked or absent from the attendance register. Staff names are shared across the modules, so you type each name once.

Which languages does it support?

Every screen can be switched between English, Hindi and Telugu, so your staff can work in the language they read best.
