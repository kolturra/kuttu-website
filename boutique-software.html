What it does for you

Measurements that are always to hand

Pick the gender, tap the garment, enter the measurements. A blouse, churidar and salwar for the same visit file under one order number, and two churidars on one order each keep their own measurements.

Blouse style and workings

Record neckline, back style, sleeves, construction, padding and lining, and order blouse workings such as aari, maggam, embroidery, zari, stone, beads, cut work or tassels in any combination.

Saree services and alterations

Saree fall, pico, blouse cutting and border work go in as a service order. An alteration is a garment of its own, with no body measurements needed.

Advances and balance, always right

Add each advance with its date, mode and transaction ID, edit or delete it later, and see what is still due on every order and in a Balance Due list.

Cutting slips and clean customer bills

Print cutting and tailoring slips for the workshop. The customer's bill stays clean: measurements are on the slip, not on their copy.

GST bills and WhatsApp

Bills carry your shop name, taxable value and CGST/SGST, can be printed, and open in WhatsApp with the bill ready for you to send.

Questions

Can Kuttu store customer measurements for a boutique?

Yes. Measurements are saved per garment under each order, with a customer rating (New, Regular, VIP or Premium), so a repeat customer's details are quick to find.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Kuttu runs on your own Windows PC and keeps your records there.

Is it only for boutiques?

No. The same suite serves tailoring units, saree shops, readymade stores, showrooms, wholesalers and factories. Each business uses the modules that fit its work.
