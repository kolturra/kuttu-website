Questions

Can I keep saree stock piece by piece?

Yes. Saree Ledger records each saree as a piece with its fabric, cost, price and barcode, and Saree Sale reduces the stock when you bill it.

Does the bill show GST?

Yes. When your shop has a GSTIN, the bill shows taxable value with CGST and SGST. With no GSTIN entered, no GST is charged.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Kuttu runs on your own Windows PC and works without internet.
